# Local SEO Quality Protocol

## Truth before optimization

Read `.agents/SEO_PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`.
Unknown means unknown.

Never transform assumptions into business claims.

## Location architecture

Use the smallest number of pages necessary to satisfy distinct local searcher needs.

Do not mass-generate near-identical city/district pages.

## NAP

Where a business name/address/phone is displayed, identify inconsistencies.
Do not invent an address for a service-area business.

## Reviews

Only display/index/mark up reviews that are genuinely sourced and permitted.
Never create review or AggregateRating schema from invented testimonials.

## Local schema

Schema must describe the real business and visible content.
Do not put every possible area into structured data merely for keywords.

## Project workflow

SOURCE_OBSERVED means existing source content, not business verification. Service-location routes and general Ankara coordinates do not prove real service coverage or business address. Keep UNKNOWN facts out of newly published claims.
