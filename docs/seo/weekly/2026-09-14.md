## Content and competitors

The two live homepages now share the same exact price bands and materially similar K3, insurer, fleet and fixed-price claims. This conflicts with the operating rule to keep the brands distinct and creates a doorway/helpful-content risk; it is not a penalty diagnosis. Google's relevant guidance: [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) and [people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
